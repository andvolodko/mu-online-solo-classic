Essential Controls & Hotkeys

Movement & Combat
  • Left ClickMove, pick up items, interact with NPCs
  • Right ClickCast selected spell or combat skill
  • Auto ButtonToggle AFK auto-attack (continuous combat skill)
  • Ctrl + ClickPlayer vs Player (PvP) attack force mode
Interface Windows
  • CCharacter Stats & Attributes Window
  • I or VInventory & Equipment Window
  • PParty Management Window
  • GGuild Management Window
  • EnterOpen Chat Bar / Command Input
  • EscOptions Menu / Close active dialogs
Potions & Skill Hotkeys
  • QQuick drink Health Potion (HP)
  • WQuick drink Mana Potion (MP)
  • EQuick drink Stamina / Antidote Potion
  • Ctrl + Q/W/EAssign hovered inventory potion to Q, W, or E
  • 1 .. 0Activate assigned skill
  • Ctrl + 0-9Bind highlighted skill to number hotkey
Map, Radar & Loot
  • SpacePick up nearest dropped item or Zen
  • AltToggle / Highlight all item names on ground
  • TabToggle Minimap overlay radar
  • MOpen Fast Move / Map Warp destination menu
  • HOpen Event Timers (Blood Castle, Devil Square)

In-Game Chat Commands (Press Enter to type)

Type these commands directly into the in-game chat box to boost your solo progression.

Command Syntax / Arguments Function & Description
/str /str <points> Instantly add unassigned points into Strength
/str auto /str auto <target> Auto-allocates points into Strength upon level up
/agi /agi <points> Instantly add unassigned points into Agility
/vit /vit <points> Instantly add unassigned points into Vitality
/ene /ene <points> Instantly add unassigned points into Energy
/reset /reset Reset character once max level is reached for bonus stat points
/reset auto /reset auto <S> <A> <V> <E> Enable automated reset loop with target stat distribution
/grandreset /grandreset Perform Grand Reset after reaching reset milestones
/pkclear /pkclear Cleanse PK (Player Killer / Outlaw) status back to normal
/clearinv /clearinv Wipe unequipped items in inventory bags (equipped gear is safe)
/move /move <Town/Map> Teleport to Lorencia, Devias, Noria, LostTower, Atlans, Tarkan, etc.
/post /post <message> Global broadcast message to world channel

Cryptographic Vault & Save Management

💾
Instant & Periodic Auto-Save:

Your characters, inventories, stats, and warehouse vaults are automatically saved every 10 minutes, as well as on character reset and logout. You can also click the Save button in the top bar anytime to force an instant save.

🔒
AES-256-GCM Encryption:

All save files are stored in your browser's persistent LocalStorage, protected with military-grade AES-GCM-256 encryption and SHA-256 tamper-proof checksum verification.

📤
Exporting Backups:

Click the Export icon in the top bar to download an encrypted backup file (.musav). Keep this safe so you never lose your hard-earned items and resets.

📥
Importing on Any Device:

Click Import to restore a previously downloaded save file. The game will automatically load your data and reload the WebAssembly client smoothly.

Classic 0.97k Equipment Upgrades & Secrets

Jewels & Upgrades
  • Jewel of Bless: Upgrades items from +0 up to +6 with a 100% success rate.
  • Jewel of Soul: Upgrades items from +7 to +9. Base success is 50%, increased to 75% for items with Luck!
  • Jewel of Life: Adds additional damage or defense options (up to +16). 50% success rate.
  • Chaos Machine: Located in Noria (180, 102). Used for crafting +10 / +11 items and Level 1 / Level 2 Wings!
Beginner Progression Guide
  • Early Levels (1-20): Hunt Spiders, Budge Dragons, and Hounds outside Lorencia or Noria town gates.
  • Mid Levels (20-80): Move to Devias (Worms, Ice Monsters, Yetis) or Dungeon for higher EXP and Jewel drops.
  • Lost Tower & Atlans (80-160): Hunt Shadow, Balrog, and Bahamut. High chance of Excellent weapon and armor drops.
  • Tarkan & Death Beam Knight (160+): Endgame solo hunting ground for Mutant, Iron Wheel, and Death Beam Knight.

Technology & Architecture
  • WebAssembly Monolith: C++17 GameServer & WebGL engine compiled directly into WASM via Emscripten.
  • Zero Latency (0 ms): In-memory binary packet channel running in dedicated Web Worker threads at 20 TPS.
  • Cryptographic Local Vault: AES-256-GCM encryption with SHA-256 integrity checks for offline character and warehouse saves.
